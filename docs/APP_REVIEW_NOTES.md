# App Review notes (Micro Jam)

Answers to Apple's Guideline 2.1 information request (October 2026). Also saved in App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes. Paste the text below into the reply in App Review, with the screen recording attached.

```
1. Screen recording: attached to our reply in App Review (recorded on a physical iPhone on the latest iOS, starting from app launch).

2. Purpose and audience
Micro Jam: Bus Park Puzzle is a single-player casual puzzle game for all ages (4+). The bus park is jammed: the player taps buses to drive them out of the lot in the right order, sends each one to a loading bay, and matching passengers board by colour. When a bus is full it leaves. The challenge is planning the order before the loading bays fill up. Levels are endless and get gradually busier, with simple twists (sleeping drivers, roadwork cones, mystery buses, padlocks, a tunnel). Its value is a relaxing, thoughtful puzzle in short sessions with no timers and no pressure, playable offline.

3. Setup and main features
No account, login or sign-up. Install, open and play; nothing else is needed. Levels 1-3 and 6 have guided tutorials (a hand shows what to tap). Main features:
- Home: the route map. Tap PLAY to start the next level.
- In a level: tap a bus to drive it out. Boosters at the bottom (Crane, Extra Bay, Shuffle) help when stuck.
- Daily Jam and Daily Tasks: buttons on the left of Home; a new free puzzle and three easy tasks every day.
- Shop (left tab): boosters, lives and cosmetic bus paints, horns and themes, bought with in-game coins earned by playing.
- Profile (right tab): an editable display name (stored only on the device), stats and achievements.
- Settings (gear, top right): sound, music, vibration, hints, privacy policy, ad privacy options and credits.
All content is unlocked by playing. Nothing requires payment, and there are no in-app purchases.

4. External services
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when the player taps a "Watch" button for a bonus (free coins, a booster, an extra life, or doubled coins or gifts). There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads between levels.
- Google User Messaging Platform: the ad consent form where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and the IDFA explainer shown before Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
The app has no user accounts, no in-app purchases, no payment processing, no AI services and no server of its own. Progress is saved only on the device. The game is fully playable offline.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. The only difference is legally required: players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's ad consent form. The app is in English.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. All art, sound and text were created for the game; all towns, buses and people are fictional.

User-generated content: none is shared between players (no chat, multiplayer or public profiles). The display name is stored only on the device.
```
